Norway's richest fund just said half their staff now codes. Should you be scared?

Nicolai Tangen runs $2 TRILLION of Norway's money — and he just dropped a truth bomb that nobody's talking about enough. ☕

🎙️
The Nordic Hustle
Oslo · 30 March 2026
⏱ 3 min read

OK so imagine you're at a breakfast event in Oslo. You grab your coffee. And then the guy who controls more money than most countries looks at the room and basically says: the world has already changed. You just haven't caught up yet.

That's exactly what happened when Nicolai Tangen — CEO of the Norwegian Oil Fund — spoke at a seminar this morning. His message? Half of the people working at Norway's $2 trillion fund are now coding. Not just the IT guys. Half. The whole place.

And look — this isn't some tech bro on Twitter telling you to "learn to code or get left behind." This is the person managing Norway's financial future, basically confirming what a lot of us have been feeling: AI is not coming. It's already here. And the people at the top? They moved fast.

"We are in the middle of the biggest shift in modern history. And it's wild that we get to live through it."

— Heard at the Oslo seminar, 30 March 2026

So what does this actually mean for you? Especially if you're young, if you're a woman trying to break into business or tech, if you're self-taught and nobody handed you connections on a silver plate?

✨ What this actually means for you
🚀
The gatekeepers are losing their grip. AI tools mean you can build things that used to need an entire team.
💡
Self-taught is no longer a weakness — it's literally the playbook the smartest funds are running.
⏰
The window is open right now. Companies are desperate for people who can move fast with AI.
🎯
You don't need to be a software engineer. You need to know how to use the tools. That's learnable in weeks.

The people who are going to win in the next five years aren't necessarily the ones with the best degrees. They're the ones who adapted while everyone else was still debating whether AI was real.

Tangen isn't guessing. He's already placed the bet. The question is — what bet are you placing?
